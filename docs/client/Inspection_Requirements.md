\# Inspection Requirements



\## Vision Inspectable



\- Presence of grooves

\- Chamfer

\- Outer Diameter (OD)

\- Inner Diameter (ID)

\- Overall Length

\- Surface defects (Scratch, Burr, Crack, Damage)



\## Not Vision Inspectable



\- Heat Treatment (55–45 HRC)

\- Minimum Case Depth

\- Copper Underplate Thickness

\- Nickel Plating Thickness

\- Salt Spray Resistance



\## Project Note



This document is the single source of truth for inspection requirements.

All future inspection rules must follow the client drawing or later client clarification.
